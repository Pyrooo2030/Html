Tree of Wisdom

Current stage: Seed

Every tree starts as a seed. As you progress through your learning journey, your tree will grow and flourish.

How To Grow Your Tree

1

Seed

The kind of seed you'll have depends on what language you first choose to learn.

2

Root And Trunk

Once you have a solid foundation in your chosen language (Mastered or Passed an advance or professional level coding test), your tree will develop strong roots and a sturdy trunk.

3

Branches

As you learn different kinds of coding languages (Assuming you mastered your first language), your tree will grow branches, each representing the new language you are learning.

4

Leaves

As your tree continues to grow, the leaves grown will represent all of the achievements and accomplishments you have gained throughout your learning journey.
