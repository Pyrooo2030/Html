The Problem: Cafe Receipt

You are making a small program for a cafe. It asks the customer for their name, the price of one item and how many they want. Then it prints a receipt with the subtotal, the tax and the total.

Requirements

  1. Create a constant for the tax rate and set it to 0.10.
  2. Ask the user for their name, the item price and the quantity using Console.ReadLine().
  3. Convert the price to a double and the quantity to an int.
  4. Store the subtotal, tax and total in variables.
  5. Print the receipt using Console.WriteLine().
  6. Add at least one comment that explains part of your code.

Sample Run

If the user types Alex, 50 and 2, your program should print:

Enter your name: Alex
Enter item price: 50
Enter quantity: 2
--- Receipt ---
Customer: Alex
Subtotal: 100
Tax: 10
Total: 110
